Know the Market

Who buys from Boulder businesses?

Three groups overlap here: long-time residents, a large university community and a workforce built around research, aerospace and health care.

The 2020 census counted 108,250 people in the City of Boulder, the county seat of Boulder County, and the 2024 estimate is 106,803. The wider metro area held 330,758 people in 2020. CU Boulder alone accounts for roughly 46,000 residents, counting about 30,000 undergraduates, 7,000 graduate students and 10,000 faculty and staff.

Get the Basics Right

What does the City of Boulder require before you market?

Boulder is a home-rule city that runs its own sales tax system, and the City says plainly that a business license is required for any business operating in Boulder. That includes service businesses whether or not their services are taxable, and businesses based elsewhere that send staff into the city or make more than one delivery there in a year. A business with more than one Boulder location needs a separate license for each one, and there is a filing requirement even when the tax due is $0.

Why does this belong in a marketing guide? Three reasons:

  • Your name should match everywhere. The business name on your license, your Google Business Profile, your website and your directory listings should be the same. Mismatches confuse customers and the search engines that try to verify you.
  • Events need their own license. If you sell at or sponsor a special event and are not already licensed in Boulder, the City offers a Special Event Taxpayer license, and the City notes that a license is required even when no sales are made. Sort that out before you book the booth.
  • Each location gets its own profile. Just as each location needs its own license, each storefront that serves customers in person should have its own Google Business Profile with its own hours and photos.

If you are starting from scratch, the City's “Starting a Business in Boulder” page walks through the steps. Licensing questions go to the City's Sales Tax office.

Timing

How should you plan around Boulder's calendar?

Few Colorado cities have a year shaped as much by one institution and a handful of signature events.

The academic year. When thousands of students arrive in August, demand for housing, furniture, bikes, phone repair, haircuts, food delivery and part-time staffing all rises. When they leave in May, campus-adjacent businesses slow down while graduation weekend brings families who need hotels, restaurants and gifts. If you sell near campus or on University Hill, plan two seasons, not one.

Bolder Boulder. The 10K has been held every Memorial Day since 1979 and draws more than 50,000 runners, joggers, walkers and wheelchair racers. Running stores, physical therapists, massage and recovery studios, brunch spots and coffee shops near the course should plan offers and Google Posts for the weeks before race day.

Festivals and film. Boulder already hosts the Boulder International Film Festival, the Colorado Music Festival and the Boulder Bach Festival, and the Sundance Film Festival is moving to Boulder starting in 2027. That brings a new wave of visitors searching for places to eat, stay and shop. Restaurants, hotels, transportation, retail and personal services should have accurate hours, current photos and clear booking options in place well before the festival arrives.

Outdoor seasons. The city protects tens of thousands of acres of open space, and the League of American Bicyclists recognizes it as a Platinum Bicycle Friendly Community. Bike shops, outfitters, guides and outdoor-gear retailers see demand follow the weather. If customers can ride to you, say so: mention bike parking in your profile and on your site.

Trust

What do Boulder customers expect from a business online?

Boulder residents care about how businesses operate, not just what they sell. That is an opportunity, as long as your claims hold up. Words like “eco-friendly,” “sustainable,” “non-toxic” or “carbon neutral” can attract customers here, but the Federal Trade Commission's Green Guides expect environmental marketing claims to be specific and backed up. “We use low-VOC paint on every interior job” is a claim a customer can check. “Green cleaning” on its own is not.

  • Be specific. Name the product, certification or practice behind any environmental or health claim.
  • Show your people. Real photos of your team and your work build more trust than stock images.
  • Publish prices or ranges where you can. Careful buyers appreciate knowing what to expect before they call.
  • Answer reviews thoughtfully. A calm, specific reply to a critical review often impresses readers more than another five stars.

AI Search

How can a Boulder business get recommended by AI assistants?

The Pew Research Center reported in 2025 that 34% of U.S. adults had used ChatGPT, and plenty of people now ask an assistant or read Google's AI Overviews before they scroll through links. These tools build their answers from information they can find and trust: your website, your Google Business Profile, reviews and reputable directories. You cannot buy your way into those answers, but you can make your business easy to understand and easy to verify.

  • Put the basics on your website in plain text: what you do, where you serve, hours, prices or ranges and how to book.
  • Answer the questions customers really ask, in short, direct paragraphs.
  • Keep your business name, phone number and address identical across every listing.
  • Earn reviews that describe the specific service and, where it fits naturally, the part of Boulder you served.
  • Add structured data so search engines can read your business details reliably.

This is the same discipline that helps you rank in Google Maps, which Google says depends on relevance, distance and prominence. Do it once, properly, and it supports both.

Your First 60 Days

Where should a Boulder business start?

Weeks 1–2: Confirm your City license is current, then make your Google Business Profile, website and top directories show the identical business name, phone number and hours.

Weeks 3–4: Rewrite your main service pages to answer real customer questions, add genuine photos and put a tap-to-call button on every page.

Weeks 5–6: Start a consistent review routine and reply to every review, old and new.

Weeks 7–8: Mark the CU calendar, Bolder Boulder and your industry's busy season on a planning calendar, then schedule Google Posts and any paid campaigns a month ahead of each one.

Boulder Marketing Questions

Quick answers for Boulder owners

How should a small business market itself in Boulder, CO?
Focus on proof: a complete Google Business Profile, accurate listings, genuine reviews, real photos and specific claims customers can verify. Then time campaigns around the CU academic year and major events such as Bolder Boulder.
Do I need a City of Boulder business license to advertise there?
The City of Boulder says a business license is required for any business operating in Boulder, including many service businesses based elsewhere that work in the city. Check with the City Sales Tax office for your situation.
How big is the Boulder market?
The 2020 census counted 108,250 people in the City of Boulder, and the metro area held 330,758. CU Boulder accounts for roughly 46,000 residents including students, faculty and staff.
When is the busiest time of year for Boulder businesses?
It depends on the business. Campus-area demand follows the August move-in and May graduation, Bolder Boulder brings crowds every Memorial Day, and the Sundance Film Festival moves to Boulder starting in 2027.
Can I call my Boulder business eco-friendly in ads?
Only if you can back it up. The FTC Green Guides expect environmental claims to be specific and supported, so name the product, practice or certification behind the claim.
How do I get my Boulder business recommended by ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?
Make your business easy to understand and verify: clear details on your website, consistent listings, helpful answers to common questions, structured data and reviews that describe real work. No one can guarantee placement in AI answers.
